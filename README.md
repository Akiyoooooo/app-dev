# 🛸 Favorite Series Guide: Stranger Things

> "Friends don't lie." — *Eleven*

## 📌 Overview
Stranger Things is an American science fiction horror drama television series created by the Duffer Brothers. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, the series centers around the disappearance of a young boy and the appearance of a girl with psychokinetic abilities, leading to secrets involving secret government experiments and supernatural forces.

## 🎭 Main Characters
Here are some of the primary characters and their respective roles:
* **Eleven ("El")** — A young girl with powerful telepathic and psychokinetic abilities.
* **Mike Wheeler** — An intelligent, dedicated student and loyal friend to Will, Dustin, and Lucas.
* **Jim Hopper** — The Chief of the Hawkins Police Department who protects Eleven.
* **Dustin Henderson** — A quick-witted, lovable member of the party with a passion for science.
* **Lucas Sinclair** — The pragmatic and realistic voice of reason among the group.
* **Will Byers** — The boy whose mysterious disappearance triggers the events of Season 1.
